EasyLatih Trainer Collaboration Terms

Draft for implementation and legal review. Trainers should not be asked to accept this version in production until EasyLatih confirms the commercial terms, payment process, cancellation rules and legal wording.

Draft version: 6 September 2026

1. Nature of collaboration

Approval as an EasyLatih trainer collaborator allows EasyLatih to consider the trainer and approved programmes for public, in-house, online or customised training opportunities. Approval does not create an employment relationship, exclusivity, guaranteed assignment, minimum training volume or guaranteed income.

2. Programme approval

Programme ideas remain subject to EasyLatih review. EasyLatih may request amendments to title, target audience, learning outcomes, course structure, duration, pricing position or supporting information before eTRiS registration or website publication. Publication does not guarantee that a programme will be scheduled or sold.

3. Accuracy and professional standing

The trainer is responsible for ensuring submitted qualifications, experience, certificates, professional claims, course information and availability are accurate and current. Material changes that may affect suitability or compliance should be disclosed promptly.

4. Training opportunities and award

EasyLatih may broadcast an opportunity to multiple matching trainers. Trainers may indicate interest, availability, fee and remarks through the portal. EasyLatih may select a trainer based on client requirements, expertise, availability, commercial fit, past performance and other relevant considerations. Only a formal award or confirmation from EasyLatih constitutes appointment for that assignment.

5. Trainer fee and payment

Trainer fee, reimbursable expenses, invoicing requirements and payment timing for each assignment will be stated or confirmed separately for that engagement. A trainer should not assume that a proposed fee is accepted until EasyLatih confirms the appointment and commercial terms.

6. Client confidentiality and relationship

Client information, requirements, contacts, pricing, proposals and opportunity details received through EasyLatih are confidential and should be used only for the relevant training engagement. A trainer should not independently solicit or bypass EasyLatih in relation to an opportunity or client specifically introduced through EasyLatih, except where EasyLatih has agreed otherwise in writing or where an independently existing relationship has been disclosed and accepted.

7. Training materials and intellectual property

Unless otherwise agreed for a specific engagement, trainers retain ownership of original training materials they create. The trainer grants EasyLatih a limited right to use approved programme titles, descriptions, learning points, trainer biography and reasonable excerpts or previews necessary to market, propose, administer and deliver the relevant programme. Client-specific deliverables or jointly developed materials may be governed by separate written terms.

8. Professional photo, biography and marketing consent

By submitting a professional trainer photo after approval and accepting the relevant consent, the trainer authorises EasyLatih to use the submitted photo, name, professional biography and approved credentials for the trainer profile, programme pages, proposals, brochures, posters, social media and other marketing materials directly related to EasyLatih programmes or the trainer's collaboration with EasyLatih. EasyLatih should not materially alter credentials or create misleading endorsements.

9. Conduct and delivery standard

Trainers are expected to be punctual, professional, prepared, respectful of participants and clients, and to deliver the agreed content and learning outcomes. EasyLatih may retain internal performance notes and participant/client feedback for quality assurance and future trainer selection.

10. Cancellation, replacement and changes

Assignment-specific cancellation, postponement or replacement arrangements will follow the terms communicated for the relevant programme or client. Trainers should notify EasyLatih promptly if circumstances affect confirmed availability. EasyLatih may request a reasonable replacement plan or supporting handover where appropriate.

11. HRD Corp, eTRiS and compliance

Where a programme is registered, claimed or administered under HRD Corp or another scheme, the trainer agrees to provide accurate information and reasonable supporting documents required for programme registration, trainer records, attendance, assessment or audit. EasyLatih retains control over what is submitted under EasyLatih's training-provider account.

12. Personal data and documents

Trainer personal data and documents are processed according to the EasyLatih Privacy Notice. Trainers should only upload documents reasonably requested for collaboration, compliance or payment purposes.

13. Suspension and termination

Either party may stop future collaboration. EasyLatih may suspend or deactivate a trainer profile or programme for material misrepresentation, repeated quality issues, breach of confidentiality, compliance concerns, client complaints, non-response or other reasonable operational grounds. Existing confirmed engagements remain subject to their specific terms unless otherwise agreed.

14. Version and acceptance record

The portal records the version and timestamp of accepted collaboration terms. If EasyLatih makes material changes, trainers may be asked to review and accept a new version before receiving or accepting further assignments.

15. Contact

Questions about these terms may be sent to sales@easylatih.my.
