REGISTRATION, CANCELLATION & HRD CORP TERMS

1. Registration Confirmation
Registration is subject to seat availability and confirmation by Easy Latih Consultancy.

2. HRD Corp Grant Application
For HRD Corp claimable registrations, Easy Latih Consultancy will provide the necessary supporting documents and guidance to assist employers in submitting their HRD Corp grant applications.

However, the employer remains responsible for ensuring that the grant application is submitted within the timeline stipulated by HRD Corp and for obtaining the necessary approval before the programme commencement date.

Where grant approval is not obtained before the programme commencement date, the registration may be converted to a self-sponsored registration and the applicable self-sponsored fee may become payable by the employer.

3. Participant Replacement
Participant replacement is allowed at no additional charge provided written notification is received at least one (1) working day before the programme commencement date.

4. Attendance Requirement
Certificates of Attendance will only be issued to participants who attend at least seventy-five percent (75%) of the programme duration.

5. Photography and Recording
Easy Latih Consultancy reserves the right to take photographs and videos during the programme for training, marketing and documentation purposes unless otherwise requested in writing by the participant or employer.

6. Accuracy of Information
The employer is responsible for ensuring that all information submitted during registration is accurate and complete. Easy Latih Consultancy shall not be responsible for errors arising from incorrect information provided.

7. Outstanding Payment
Certificates, training materials and related documents may be withheld until full payment has been received, where applicable.

8. Programme Changes and Cancellation
Easy Latih Consultancy reserves the right to postpone, reschedule, change the trainer, venue or delivery method due to unforeseen circumstances. Registered participants will be informed accordingly.

For cancellations initiated by the employer, participant or registered attendee:

a) Cancellation made more than seven (7) working days before the programme date will not incur any cancellation charge.

b) Cancellation made between three (3) and seven (7) working days before the programme date will incur a cancellation charge equivalent to fifty percent (50%) of the programme fee.

c) Cancellation made less than three (3) working days before the programme date or no-show participants will incur a cancellation charge equivalent to one hundred percent (100%) of the programme fee.

Participant replacement is allowed in accordance with Clause 3 and will not be treated as a cancellation.

Where Easy Latih Consultancy postpones or cancels a programme, registered participants may choose either a replacement programme date or a full refund of any fees paid.
