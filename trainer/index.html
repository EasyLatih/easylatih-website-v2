How collaboration works

1. Register your account
Only basic contact and expertise details are required.
2. Pitch up to 5 active programme ideas
We review title, specific target audience, training need, learning points, duration and market fit.
3. EasyLatih reviews and comments
Your proposal may be shortlisted, returned for clarification, kept for future demand or approved to collaborate.
4. Approved trainers complete onboarding
Full trainer details, professional photo, documents, collaboration terms and full programme information are requested only after approval.
5. Receive matching training opportunities
Active trainers can receive relevant public or in-house opportunities based on category, expertise, location and availability.
Registration and programme submission do not guarantee programme approval, engagement or training assignment by EasyLatih.
